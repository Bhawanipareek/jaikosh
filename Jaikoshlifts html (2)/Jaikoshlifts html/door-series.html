Automatic Elevator Door

Automatic elevator doors are designed to ensure safe, smooth, and reliable operation without manual effort. These doors open and close automatically using a motorized mechanism synchronized with the elevator control system, providing controlled movement and enhanced passenger safety.

Manual Elevator Door

Manual elevator doors are simple, non-motorized doors that are operated by the user. These doors require manual opening and closing before and after elevator use. Manual doors are typically used in low-rise buildings, goods elevators, and cost-sensitive installations where traffic is minimal.
